Your to-do list, built around your forge and shard fusions.

"Do this now" is your to-do list in order: collect what's finished, make the best Auction House crafts, then fill the forge with what's left. The cards under it are quick looks at each tab. Forge and AH Craft are the main money makers, and the tabs after the divider are extras.

Best crafts for your free slots and coins, ranked by Score.

Ranked by Score, which blends profit per check-in, return on the coins tied up, how much capital it takes, and how easy the materials are to buy and the product is to sell (rival orders included). Click an item to see its parts and the details behind the score. A craft that finishes just before you're back beats a short one that sits idle. The suggested fill is the best mix for your free slots and coins together. Cost is buying every ingredient ready-made at instant-buy prices, and the time is that one forge step with your Quick Forge. Where forging the parts yourself pays more, the row says so. Press Start when you begin a craft in game and the page tracks the slot and counts down.

Buy a pet, upgrade it with Kat, sell it at the higher rarity.

Buy the pet, give it to Kat with the materials, sell it at the higher rarity. Kat holds one pet at a time. The sale price is the median for that rarity across all pet levels, and a low-level pet usually sells for less, so treat the profit as a ceiling. The listed pet can also sell before you get there.

Buy-order then sell-offer flips, sized to fill while you're away.

Place a buy order, then resell with a sell offer. Ranked by Score: profit per check-in, margin, how much capital it ties up, and how many rival orders sit at the top of the book. Quantities shrink as rivals grow, so the order should fill while you're away. A full flip takes two check-ins. Wide spreads can close while you're offline.

Underpriced Buy It Now listings, priced from real recent sales.

Scans every Buy It Now listing. Ranked by how often the item sells, margin, and how fresh the listing is. Listings older than Max listed minutes in Settings (60 by default) are hidden, and anything past 5 minutes is scored down. Each one is priced from the last week of sales of the same item at the same star level, capped just under the cheapest similar listing so it sells fast, plus its own upgrades (enchant books, gems, recombobulator, potato books, master stars, scrolls) at your Upgrade value %. The estimate is capped by similar listings, and anything without at least three matching recent sales is left out, so it errs low. Reforge stones count where the stone trades on the Bazaar, and items with skins or dyes are skipped.

Auctions ending soon

Normal auctions closing in the next 90 minutes where the bidding is still under what the item resells for. Bid near the end, never go past "Bid up to", and expect to lose some. Other bidders can push the price up in the last seconds, and each late bid adds time.

Buy materials on the Bazaar, craft, sell on the Auction House. No racing other flippers.

These don't depend on catching a listing in the first minute, so there are far more of them than auction snipes. Only crafts that clear your Min craft profit in Settings (3M each by default) are shown, ranked by profit per craft first, then how often the item sells, then margin. Materials are priced at instant-buy from the live order book, crafting a sub-part only when that is cheaper. The sale price is the lower of the cheapest listing and the median recent sale. Recipes you can't make are hidden: collections are checked from your public profile, and slayer and skill levels from your API key. Without a key, slayer and skill recipes stay on the list with a note. "Make" keeps you under 15% of the item's daily sales.

Shard fusions that pay more than your mining.

Your Hunting Box

Every accessory you are missing and every attribute worth maxing, ranked for you.

Accessories: reads your bag, then lists what is left. "Buy on the AH" is anything for sale that you meet the requirements for. "Recombobulate" raises one you own by a rarity, which costs no bag slot. "Earn yourself" cannot be bought. "Locked" needs a slayer, skill or other level you do not have. The full list runs free moves first, then cheapest coins per point, then accessories to earn, then locked ones; the star pins a row to the top. Prices are the lowest listing. If your bag is full, the cost of a new slot is added to each new accessory. Attributes: a hand-ranked list for Master Mode, mining and Magical Power, priced from the Bazaar.

Prices that move with the mayor. Buy what's cheap now, wait on what's pricey.

Each row is a pattern measured from 18 months of daily Bazaar prices across 112 mayor terms. It only made the list if it repeated in at least four separate terms, pointed the same way at least 80% of the time, and held in both halves of the history. "Price shift" is the typical price against the item's own normal while that mayor or perk is in office. A flip has to beat the Bazaar tax twice, so small shifts are for timing things you'd buy anyway. Hold sizes use your Hold cap in Settings and stay under a tenth of the item's daily volume. Patterns can break after a game update, and rows with few terms are weaker evidence.
